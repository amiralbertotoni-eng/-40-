A letter for my favourite person

To my Mia, my almost-everything

Some feelings are too special to fit into an ordinary message, so I wanted to give them their own little place in the world.

My lovely Mia, If I could give you one thing in this world, it would be the chance to see yourself through my eyes, even for a moment. You would see someone precious, someone worth being patient with, someone whose little ways can make an ordinary day feel special. I don't need us to have a perfect story. I want the honest one — the silly moments, the little updates, the things that make us laugh, the talks where we learn to understand each other better, and the days when we choose kindness even when things feel difficult. When I picture a future with you, I picture a thousand small moments: checking how your day went, making you smile when you need it, celebrating your wins, comforting each other through hard days, and growing into people who keep choosing each other freely. I can't promise that every day will be easy. I can promise that I want to keep learning, listening, being honest, and showing my love through what I do — not only through what I say. You are so special to me, Mia. This little website is just one tiny way of saying that my heart has made room for you in a very beautiful way. With all my love,
Your Amir ♡
